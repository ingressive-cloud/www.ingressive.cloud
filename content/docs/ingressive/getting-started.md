# Getting Started
This page takes you through getting started with Ingressive. It should take you about five minutes to get from zero to sending traffic to any private service you want. 

## Create Account
Creating an account takes just a few minutes. Ingressive will guide you through adding Domains, Connectors, and hooking them up with Sites.

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
We are currently invite-only. Please join the Discord and tell us about your project. 
{{< /callout >}}

1. Open the [Ingressive Console](https://console.ingressive.cloud). 
1. If you aren't logged in, you'll be taken to the login page. 
1. Click "Register"
1. Pick a username - this can't be changed later
1. You will need to add the two factor setup to your favourite app or password manager.
1. Log in with your username and password
1. You will be taken to the onboarding wizard
